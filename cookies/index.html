Cookie Policy

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website may store on a user's device while browsing. They may be used to ensure that the website functions correctly, remember user preferences, or provide additional services such as analytics and advertising.

2. Technical Cookies

The website may use cookies and technical technologies that are strictly necessary for the operation of the pages, security and the management of requested features. Where provided for by applicable law, consent is not required for such technologies, although appropriate information must still be provided.

3. Google AdSense

The website may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. At the time this policy is published, the service may not yet be active; this section is maintained to describe the expected functionality if AdSense is activated.

AdSense may use cookies and other storage technologies to deliver and measure advertisements, prevent fraudulent activity and, where permitted, personalise advertising based on available information and the choices expressed by the user.

For users located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google requires consent where required by law for the use of cookies or other storage technologies and for the processing of personal data necessary for personalised advertising.

If AdSense is activated, the website will implement an appropriate consent management mechanism to collect and record the user's choices before activating technologies that require consent.

For further information about Google's advertising services and privacy practices, please consult Google's page regarding partner services and Google's Privacy Policy .

4. Consent Management

When the website uses cookies or other tracking technologies for purposes other than those strictly necessary for its technical operation, their use will be subject to the user's consent where required by applicable law. Users may provide, modify or withdraw their preferences according to the mechanisms made available by the website.

5. Third-Party Cookies

The use of third-party services may result in cookies being installed or other tracking technologies being used by the relevant providers. The processing activities carried out by such providers are described in their respective privacy policies and cookie policies.

6. Browser Settings and Cookie Deletion

Users can manage or delete cookies through their browser settings. Disabling technical cookies that are necessary for the operation of the website may prevent certain parts of the website from functioning correctly.

7. Privacy Policy

For further information regarding the processing of personal data, please consult the Wrapst Privacy Policy.
